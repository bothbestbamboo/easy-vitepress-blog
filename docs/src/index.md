## 🏭 Manufacturing Process & Craftsmanship

Our state-of-the-art production facility ensures that every swim spa, hot tub, and freestanding bathtub meets the highest international standards of durability and craftsmanship.

1. **Acrylic Shell Thermoforming:** Utilizing premium Aristech acrylic sheets heated and vacuum-molded into precise ergonomic molds.
2. **Multi-Layer Fiberglass Reinforcement:** Applying high-strength resin and fiberglass backing layers to guarantee superior structural rigidity and pressure resistance.
3. **Precision Jet & Plumbing Assembly:** Expertly installing stainless steel hydro-massage jets, air injectors, and high-flow sanitary tubing with rigorous leak testing.
4. **Rigorous Quality Control Inspection:** Every unit undergoes exhaustive water-fill testing, electrical safety checks, and cosmetic inspections before final export packaging.
