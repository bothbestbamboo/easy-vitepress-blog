# 👋 Welcome to XC Spa

![XC Spa Automated Assembly Line](/assets/image/f6.jpg)

**XC Spa Co., Ltd.** is a world-class manufacturing enterprise and global leader specializing in the research, development, and large-scale production of luxury **Swim Spas, Outdoor Hot Tubs, Hydrotherapy Spas, and Freestanding Bathtubs**. Founded with a vision to bridge cutting-edge hydrotherapy engineering with premium outdoor living, XC Spa has grown into a trusted OEM/ODM manufacturing partner for international distributors, resort developers, hospitality groups, and luxury contractors worldwide.

Operating an **ISO9001:2000 certified** production base in China, we integrate industrial precision with uncompromising quality control. From premium Aristech acrylic thermoforming to multi-layer fiberglass reinforcement and rigorous water-pressure testing, every XC Spa unit is engineered to deliver lifelong stability, exceptional energy efficiency, and total body relaxation.

---

## 🏭 Advanced Manufacturing Infrastructure & Craftsmanship

Our state-of-the-art facility spans tens of thousands of square meters, functioning as a synchronized ecosystem of automated logistical rollers, precision mold forming, and strict QA testing protocols.

![Vacuum Thermoforming Frame](/assets/image/f1.jpg)

### 1. Precision Mold & Acrylic Thermoforming
We utilize genuine **Aristech Acrylic** sheets processed through large-scale, sensor-controlled vacuum thermoforming machines. Heavy-duty structural framing ensures ultra-precise shell geometry, flawless surface gloss, and superior UV/weathering resistance.

![Fiberglass Shell Reinforcement](/assets/image/f2.jpg)

### 2. Multi-Layer Fiberglass & Resin Reinforcement
To withstand high hydraulic pressure and extreme thermal fluctuations, each acrylic shell is backed with multiple layers of high-density fiberglass and eco-friendly resin. This structural reinforcement guarantees high impact strength and zero warping under full water capacity.

![Plumbing & Jet Piping Layout](/assets/image/f3.jpg)

### 3. Sanitary Plumbing & Hydrotherapy Jet Engineering
Every massage jet, air injector, and water circulation loop is manually assembled with high-grade, food-safe PVC tubing and anti-corrosion stainless steel fittings. Our veteran engineers configure targeted massage zones for ergonomic neck, back, and leg therapy.

![Automated Roller Assembly Line](/assets/image/f5.jpg)

### 4. Automated Assembly & Continuous Conveyor Lines
Our facility features multi-stage industrial conveyor roller systems, enabling efficient movement across insulation foaming, cabinet skirt fitting, electronic control integration, and final packaging—maximizing monthly output capacity for bulk container shipments.
